Clarity before purchase.

Pacific Supply Resources exists to remove friction from procurement in Guam. We are part of Yanco Corporation, with a physical presence in Harmon Industrial Park so customers can align on details in person or by phone—not only by email.

Our process is intentionally straightforward: understand the application, confirm compatible options, quote with transparent assumptions, and support delivery logistics that match your site constraints.

Teams that value documentation, repeat orders, and predictable communication. If you are consolidating vendors or standing up a new maintenance program, we can help structure lists and replenishment cadence.
