About Pacific Supply Resources

Local coordination. Serious sourcing.

Pacific Supply Resources connects Guam organizations with the parts, equipment, and consumables they rely on every day. As part of Yanco Corporation, we combine wholesale distribution with practical sourcing so facilities, contractors, and institutions can stay focused on their core work.

How we work with you

We start with your list: part numbers, photos, manufacturer names, or a description of the job. Our team clarifies specifications, proposes alternatives when originals are unavailable, and confirms pricing and timing before you commit.

What “good service” means here

  • Honest expectations on availability and lead times
  • Repeatable ordering for maintenance schedules and storerooms
  • Responsive follow-up when shipments or specifications change
